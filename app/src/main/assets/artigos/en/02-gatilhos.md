Few people relapse "out of nowhere". There is almost always a familiar script: the same time, the same place, the same state of mind. In relapse prevention that script has a name: a **high-risk situation**. Knowing yours is half the work.

## The most common triggers

- **Time and place:** late at night, in bed, in the bathroom, with the door locked.
- **State of the body:** tired, sleepy, hungry, after drinking.
- **Emotional state:** boredom, loneliness, anxiety, anger, after a frustration.
- **The device:** phone in hand with no purpose, scrolling "just a little".

Recovery groups share a simple reminder, **HALT**: hungry, angry, lonely, tired. It isn't a scientific rule, but it's a good alarm: when two or more are present, your guard tends to drop.

## The "if… then…" plan

Deciding in the moment, with the urge running high, is hard. Deciding **beforehand** is much easier. Psychologists call this an *implementation intention*: a plan shaped like "if X happens, then I do Y".

A meta-analysis gathered **94 tests** of this technique and found a medium-to-large effect (**d = 0.65**) on reaching goals. It is one of the simplest and most tested tools in behavioral psychology.

Examples:

- *If* I get into bed with my phone after 11 p.m., *then* I leave it charging outside the bedroom.
- *If* the urge hits, *then* I open the Refuge and breathe for 2 minutes before anything else.
- *If* I'm home alone at night, *then* I go out for a 10-minute walk or call someone.

## Change your surroundings

Willpower gets tired; surroundings don't. Make the old path **harder** and the new one **easier**:

- Phone outside the bedroom at bedtime.
- Website blockers turned on, with the password kept by someone else, if that helps.
- Compasso on your home screen, where the old shortcuts used to be.

## Today's exercise

Take the last three times you slipped or almost slipped and answer: **when**, **where**, **how I was feeling** and **what I'll do next time**. Turn each answer into an "if… then…". Three well-written plans are worth more than a lot of promises.
