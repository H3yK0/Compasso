When people think of triggers, sadness, stress and loneliness come to mind. But there's a quiet trigger that keeps showing up in the stories of people trying to quit pornography: **having nothing to do**.

## The shock experiment

In 2014, psychologist Timothy Wilson and colleagues published a series of **11 studies** with a seemingly simple task: spend **6 to 15 minutes** alone in a room, with no phone and no book, just with your own thoughts.

Most people **didn't enjoy** the experience and found it much more pleasant to do mundane activities. In one of the studies, participants could, if they wanted, give themselves a small electric shock during their time alone, a shock they had earlier said they would pay to avoid. **Many pressed the button** anyway.

The authors' conclusion: most of us prefer doing **something** to doing nothing, even when that something is unpleasant.

## What this has to do with urges

If sitting still with your own mind is already uncomfortable, imagine 11 p.m., alone, with a device in your pocket that delivers endless stimulation in a second. Boredom doesn't "create" sexual desire, but it opens an empty space, and the old habit is the fastest way to fill it.

That's why the path often goes like this: boredom → picking up the phone "for no reason" → scrolling → an image → the urge grows.

> Boredom isn't the enemy. The enemy is having nothing ready to put in its place.

## How to deal with boredom

1. **Build a menu before you need it.** A short list, on paper or on your phone, of things you can do in 5 minutes without a screen: go for a walk, tidy a drawer, play an instrument, do push-ups, call someone, draw.
2. **Keep the options within reach.** A book next to the couch, sneakers by the door. What's visible wins.
3. **Use the Refuge.** The games keep your attention busy with something visual for a few minutes, long enough for the wave to pass.
4. **Practice small doses of boredom.** Waiting in line without grabbing your phone, spending two minutes looking out the window. Tolerating boredom is a muscle too.
5. **Fill the gaps in your schedule.** Empty, predictable times (Sunday afternoon, the night before sleep) deserve a plan.
