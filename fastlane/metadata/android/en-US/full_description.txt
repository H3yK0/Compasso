Lodestar is a refuge for people who want to quit pornography and regain control. Everything stays on your phone: the app doesn't ask for internet access and has no ads or tracking.

<b>Control training</b>
Training based on the "stop-start" method, used for decades in sex therapy: rhythm rounds with a beat that speeds up, full pauses with guided breathing and an "I'm close" button to stop right away. Five levels (Easy, Medium, Hard, Impossible and Custom). If it doesn't work out, the "Couldn't hold on" button ends the session there and records it honestly.

<b>Smart control</b>
Fixed, visible rules space out releases (12 to 72 h interval, no rushing between sessions, a busy week doubles the interval). Loosening the rules only takes effect 24 h later. Focus mode pins the screen during the session.

<b>Kegel: the control muscle</b>
Training for the pelvic floor, which takes part in erection and ejaculation, up to 3 times a day. Hold levels (3, 5, 8 or 10 s, unlocked step by step) and time levels (2, 4 or 6 min), a vibration on each change and, if you want, discreet phone reminders: the notification only says "Lodestar".

<b>A refuge for when the urge hits</b>
Breathe for 2 minutes with 6 calm soundtracks generated live by the app (Tide, Light rain, Night forest, Singing bowls, Starry sky and Calm piano), and three original games to keep your mind busy until the wave passes.

<b>30 readings with sources</b>
Short articles about urges, habits, Kegel, relapse, shame, stress, sleep, alcohol, performance anxiety, relationships and when to seek help, each with its scientific references at the end. You can read with calm background music.

<b>Recommendations that explain why</b>
A local algorithm looks at your sessions and slips and suggests a training level and your next reading, always showing the reason. Every now and then, when you open the app, it suggests a reading (you can turn this off).

<b>Progress through consistency</b>
Points reward showing up, not "doing more": daily check-in, Kegel, breathing, reading and training within the rules. Levels from Beginner to Unbeaten, your sky for the month, charts and records.

<b>Privacy</b>
Anonymous codename, optional PIN (with a growing wait after wrong attempts), no cloud backup and no device-to-device transfer, history that can be turned off or erased.

The app is a support tool and doesn't replace a health professional. In English, Portuguese and Spanish.
